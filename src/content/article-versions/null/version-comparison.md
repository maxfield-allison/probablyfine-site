## Research and factual additions in version 3

Max confirmed during this revision: “I did read the papers”. His reading is therefore part of the author-supplied record. These explanations identify the assistant’s editorial additions and evidentiary checks without recasting his research as work the assistant originated. The exact added prose is in the V2 → V3 diff.
