HOW A RUN WORKS

the fixer · read once, then forget it Goal: reach the objective host, take what you came for, then reach EXIT. Both are listed top-right. Big vaults take a 2-tick channel: siphon once per turn for two turns while holding the node — playing cards is fine, moving resets it.

Moving: click any cyan-outlined node next to you. Every hop costs a cycle.

Cards: click one in your hand to run it. Red pips = how much noise it makes; noise raises your trace. Breakers kill ICE and rival rigs — if two targets are in range you'll be asked to click one.

Red shapes: a solid hex marked ⊘ is a gate — it blocks the hop until you kill it. Dashed rings (sentries) and diamonds (patrols) are hostile but enterable — they cost trace, not passage.

Trace is the bar up top. Hit the magenta ceiling and this operator is burned. The ceiling isn't always 100% — the hotter the handle, the less room you get.

You can win loud or quiet — both are real strategies. LOUD doors are closer to the target (speed); QUIET doors start you cold (safety). Every win builds the handle\u2019s reputation; a clean exit (finish under the trace & noise limit — possible from either door) pays double reputation and cools your Heat −6. Loud-and-fast or slow-and-spotless both work; loud-and-slow is how you burn.

CHOOSE YOUR WAY IN

The door you pick shapes the board. Loud is fast and central; quiet is slow and costs tempo — but the corp barely feels it. (In the full game, hitting a corp the same way twice sharpens their file on you.)
